Two invention disclosures approved by Virginia Tech in April 2025 — DAB-CPS (INV2025-123) and LLM-Based Autonomous Building Operations (INV2025-121) — covering foundational systems developed through BUILT Lab research.

Our paper "Smart Building Operations and Virtual Assistants Using LLM" has been accepted to the 33rd ACM Symposium on the Foundations of Software Engineering (FSE 2025), June 23–27, Trondheim, Norway.

Presented "Autonomous Building Operations and Virtual Assistants Using Generative AI" as part of the prestigious 2025 Paul E. Torgersen Graduate Student Research Excellence Award at Virginia Tech, Blacksburg, VA.

Journal Article Published in IS&eBM — Decentralized Autonomous Organizations in Built Environments

Our paper on DAOs in the built environment — exploring applications, potentials, and limitations — was published in Information Systems and eBusiness Management Journal (2025).

BUILT Lab presented two papers at CRC 2024 in Des Moines, Iowa: the DT-DAO integration framework for smart building FM and the NFT-powered data exchange framework for AEC digital twins.
