RL

Reachsak Ly

Assistant Professor · School of Technology · Eastern Illinois University

Reachsak Ly is an Assistant Professor in the School of Technology at Eastern Illinois University. His research focuses on AI for the built environment, spanning LLM-based smart building systems, digital twins, edge AI, extended reality (XR), blockchain-based decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs), and GenAI-powered human–robot collaboration for construction technology education.
