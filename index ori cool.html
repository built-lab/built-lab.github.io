// About the Lab

Who We Are

The BUILT Lab (Built Environment Intelligent Systems Lab) is an academic research group dedicated to transforming how humans design, build, operate, and experience the built environment through artificial intelligence. We sit at a rare intersection of civil engineering, computer science, and human factors — producing work that is both theoretically rigorous and immediately deployable on real construction sites, in classrooms, and inside smart buildings.

Our work spans Extended Reality (AR/VR) for safety training and inspection, AI-powered adaptive tutoring for construction engineering students, human–building interaction sensing, real-time digital twins, edge AI for on-site monitoring, multimodal large language models for AEC document intelligence, and LLM-guided human–robot collaboration in unstructured job-site environments. We partner closely with industry leaders, government agencies, and global academic institutions to translate our research into tools that move the needle for the construction sector.
