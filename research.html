// 02 — Research

Areas & Projects

Eight interconnected research thrusts advancing intelligence in the built environment — from smart buildings to construction job sites.

01

XR for Construction

AR/VR for safety training, remote inspection, BIM visualization, and design review in AEC workflows.

02

AI for Construction Education

Adaptive learning, simulation-based training, RAG chatbots, and GenAI tools for AEC programs.

03

Human–Building Interaction

LLM and IoT-based systems enabling occupants to naturally interact with and control smart building environments.

04

Digital Twins

BIM-linked real-time digital twins with LLM query interfaces, anomaly detection, and facility management insights.

05

Edge AI & Sensing

On-device AI inference on edge hardware (Raspberry Pi) for smart homes, worker safety, and environmental monitoring.

06

Multimodal LLMs for AEC

Vision-language models for construction site safety monitoring, BIM querying, and document intelligence.

07

Human–Robot Collaboration

GenAI-powered human-robot collaboration demonstrations for construction technology education and job-site tasks.

08

Blockchain & DAO Systems

Decentralized autonomous organizations and blockchain-based frameworks for smart building operations and facilities governance.
